### Highlight: IoT × XR × Generative AI, co-developed with ZEBRA
We announced new technology using **XR and generative AI**, co-developed with ZEBRA, the Japanese writing instrument maker.
At its core is our **ultra-compact IoT device with Bluetooth and sensors**, built into a pen and linked with XR/AI.

That core technology works well beyond pens. Together with ZEBRA, we are looking for partners who want to turn **their ideas into real products quickly and affordably**.
Let's combine our strengths and build products and services that are meaningful and exciting.
